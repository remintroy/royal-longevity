# Highlights / The essentials

The landing-page section links directly to the published catalogue. The three main cards lead to Salon & Hair, Facial Treatments, and Spa & Wellness category pages. Six supporting rows link to Standard Facial, Hair Cut & Styling, Manicure, Massage, Yoga Classes, and Reformer Pilates. The central brand panel links to the complete services listing, where all nine categories and published services are available.

Labels and descriptions derive from the existing catalogue through `data/highlights.ts`. Destinations preserve English/Arabic locale. All cards and rows use Next Link, visible directional arrows, hover feedback, and keyboard focus outlines. RTL mirrors arrows and flex relationships. Existing layout, brand tokens, spacing and mobile touch targets are preserved. The component remains server-rendered with no additional client JavaScript.
